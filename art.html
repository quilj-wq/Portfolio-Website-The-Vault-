Art

Mona Lisa

Artist: Leonardo da Vinci

Year: 1503-1506

The Mona Lisa is a portrait by Leonardo da Vinci that's famous for the woman's mysterious expression. A lot of people are drawn to it because her smile seems to change depending on how you look at it.

Starry Night

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

The Starry Night shows a quiet town under a wild, swirling night sky. Van Gogh used bold colors and movement to make the sky feel almost alive.

Liberty Leading the People

Artist: Eugène Delacroix

Year: 1830

This painting shows Liberty leading a group of people forward during the French Revolution of 1830. It feels dramatic and powerful because it mixes a real historical event with the idea of freedom.

The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Artist: Katsushika Hokusai

Year: 1830-1832

This print shows a huge wave towering over small boats, with Mount Fuji in the background. The contrast between the giant wave and the tiny people makes nature feel incredibly powerful.
